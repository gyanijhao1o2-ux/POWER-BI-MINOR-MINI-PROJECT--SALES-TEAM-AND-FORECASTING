# POWER BI Minor Mini Project – Sales Team and Forecasting

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a Power BI-based data analysis and visualization project focused on **Sales Team Performance and Sales Forecasting**.

The main objective of this project is to analyze sales data, understand the performance of different sales team members, identify important sales trends, and use forecasting techniques to estimate future sales.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Compare the performance of different sales team members.
- Identify top-performing sales representatives.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Understand sales by different categories/regions.
- Create interactive dashboards using Microsoft Power BI.
- Forecast future sales based on historical sales data.
- Generate useful insights to support business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Sales Forecasting**
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** (Data Source)

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Sales Growth
- Sales by Sales Representative
- Sales by Region
- Monthly/Yearly Sales Trends
- Top and Bottom Performers
- Sales Performance Comparison
- Future Sales Forecast
- Interactive filters and slicers

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The project analyzes:

1. **Sales Team Performance**
   - Comparison of sales representatives.
   - Identification of high-performing and low-performing team members.

2. **Sales Trends**
   - Monthly and yearly sales analysis.
   - Identification of increasing or decreasing sales trends.

3. **Regional Analysis**
   - Comparison of sales across different regions.
   - Identification of regions contributing the most to overall sales.

4. **Sales Forecasting**
   - Historical sales data is used to identify patterns and trends.
   - Forecasting is used to estimate potential future sales.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard provides an interactive and easy-to-understand visualization of sales performance and forecasting.

Users can interact with different charts, graphs, filters, and slicers to explore the data and obtain meaningful insights.

## 💡 Expected Outcomes

This project helps in:

- Understanding sales team performance.
- Identifying sales trends and patterns.
- Making data-driven business decisions.
- Improving sales strategies.
- Estimating future sales.
- Identifying areas that require improvement.
